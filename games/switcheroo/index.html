Switcheroo

keep your eye on the marbles

A marble drops into each tumbler, then the tumblers turn over and switch places. When they stop, find each colour the counter asks for: click or tap its tumbler, or press its number. A wrong pick costs one of your three lives. Every round cleared is a level up, with more tumblers and quicker hands, then pairs switching at once and empty tumblers mixed in.
