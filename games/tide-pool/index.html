Tide Pool

match three at low tide

Swap two neighbours to line up three or more of a kind, across or down: scallops, starfish, urchins, sea glass, periwinkles or pebbles. The line washes away, the rest settle and the tide brings more; a new line made by the settling counts double, then triple. A swap that makes no line doesn't stick.
