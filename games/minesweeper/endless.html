How to play

The minefield scrolls down toward the red line. When a row touches it, every mine in that row has to be flagged 🚩 — an unflagged mine goes off and the run is over.

Flags have to be right: a 🚩 on a safe tile that reaches the line also ends the run, so you can't just flag everything. Dig safe tiles to reveal numbers, same as classic — a number counts the mines in its eight neighbours, including the rows above and below. Digging a mine ends the run too. You get one life.

The bottom rows start open, so there's something to read right away. The field starts moving on your first dig or flag, and it speeds up (and gets denser) the longer you last. Your score is how many rows you get past the line.

Mouse: left-click digs, right-click flags, click a finished number to chord. Touch: tap digs, long-press flags (or switch to 🚩 Tap flags). Keyboard: arrows move, Space digs, F flags, N restarts, P/Esc pauses.
