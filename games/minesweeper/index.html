How to play

Some squares hide mines. Dig a square to open it: a number tells you how many of its eight neighbours are mines. Open every safe square to win — you never need to flag, but it helps. Your first dig is always safe and always opens a patch of the board.

Mouse: left-click digs, right-click plants a 🚩. Click a number whose flags are all placed (or press both buttons on it, or the middle button) to chord — that opens all its other neighbours at once. A wrong flag means a chord can blow up, so be sure.

Touch: tap digs, long-press flags. Switch ⛏️ Tap digs to 🚩 Tap flags to swap them. Tap a finished number to chord.

Keyboard: click the board (or Tab to it), then arrow keys move, Space/Enter digs or chords, F flags, N starts a new game, P/Esc pauses.
