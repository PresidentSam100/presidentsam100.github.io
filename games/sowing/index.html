Sowing

mancala on a carved board

Lift every seed from one of your pits (the near row) and sow them one to a pit, round to the right and on along the far row, into your store on the way but never into the other one. The last seed in your store: sow again. The last in an empty pit of yours: take it and every seed across from it. When a row runs dry the game ends, each row's seeds going to its own store; the bigger store wins.
