Slither

Eat food to grow, and don't crash into walls, obstacles, or a body (yours or theirs). Play classic, bring a friend for local 2-player, or take on the Labyrinth.

Legend & twists
🍎 Food — eat it to grow one segment and speed up slightly.
▦ Obstacles — solid blocks scattered across the board; crashing into one ends your run.
🌀 Portals — two color-matched pairs (orange↔orange, purple↔purple); enter one end, exit the other end of the same color still moving the same direction. They jump to new spots every third time a snake goes through one.
🐍🐍 2-Player — share the board and the food. Crash into a wall, obstacle, yourself, or the other snake and you're out. Head-on collisions kill both.
Labyrinth legend
🍎 → 🚪 — eat every red apple to unlock the arch, then reach it before the clock runs out. A locked arch is a wall. Crash and you retry the level instantly — no lives.
✦ Blink apple — one teleport: click (or tap) any open cell and your head jumps there.
👻 Ghost apple — fills the ghost meter. Hold G, right-click, or 👻 to pass through walls, doors and bodies. Ghosts can't use the exit.
🍏 Fast apple — a permanent speed-up for this level. Hold Shift (or ⚡) any time to dash.
🌀 Portals — matching colors are linked; you keep your heading.
🧊 Ice — you can't turn on it. Queue your turn for when you slide off.
🟧 Doors & switches — rolling over a switch flips every door: shut ones open, open ones shut.
🔺 Spikes — they rise and fall on a beat, flashing red just before rising. Raised spikes cut any part of you.
✂ Cutter — trims whoever enters it down to three segments.
⬛ Darkness — snakes vanish in it. Apples don't.
🐍 Enemies — wanderers roam, hunters try to cut you off, munchers race you for apples (apples they eat still count). Your head hitting them kills you; their head hitting you kills them.
