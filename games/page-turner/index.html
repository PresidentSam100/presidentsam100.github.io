Page Turner

a bookworm's escape

Pages fall flat one after another, with some pictures cut right through. Each page's shadow shows its holes bright where they'll land: get wholly inside one to go through, or be squished. Worms can share a hole, and shove each other out. Pages come faster, with fewer, smaller holes.
