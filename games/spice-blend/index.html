Spice Blend

name the seller's secret blend

The seller has a blend in mind: a row of bowls with a spice in each, a spice maybe in more than one. Fill a row of your own and taste it. Each brass weight is a spice in its right bowl; each wooden weight a right spice in a wrong bowl (the weights don't say which). Name the blend before the tastes run out.
