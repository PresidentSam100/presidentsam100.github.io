8

Corner Pocket

8-ball pool · pot your group, then sink the 8

Opponent
CPU

 

How to play
  • Aim: press on the table and drag — the cue ball heads for your finger. Fine-tune with the ribbed wheel, the mouse wheel or ← →.
  • Shoot: pull the cue down the power bar and let go, or hold Space and release.
  • Spin: tap the cue ball icon to strike above centre (follow), below (draw) or to the side (english). WASD work too.
  • Break from behind the line. The table is open until someone pots a ball — that's their group, solids or stripes.
  • Fouls give the other player ball in hand anywhere: scratching, missing everything, hitting the wrong ball first, or no ball reaching a cushion.
  • Cleared your group? Tap a pocket to call the 8, then sink it there to win. The 8 early, the 8 with a scratch or the 8 in the wrong pocket loses.

You win!
