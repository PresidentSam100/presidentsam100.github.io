Fish‑A‑Fish

Whack‑a‑mole, gone fishing.

Every bobber has a key (or tap a spot). Ripples mean a fish is sniffing; when the bobber plunges, strike its key. Striking during the ripples scares it off, an empty spot splashes −2, and the old boot isn't worth hooking (−5). Five catches in a row double your points; ten triple them. On the whole keyboard, when a number's tag turns gold and shows its symbol (! % &…), only Shift + that key hooks it.
