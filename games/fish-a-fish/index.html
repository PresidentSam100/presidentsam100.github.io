Fish‑A‑Fish

Whack‑a‑mole, gone fishing.

Nine bobbers, nine keys — Q W E / A S D / Z X C (or tap a spot). Ripples mean a fish is sniffing; when the bobber plunges, strike its key. Striking during the ripples scares it off, an empty spot splashes −2, and the old boot isn't worth hooking (−5). Five catches in a row double your points; ten triple them. Full Pond plays the whole keyboard — and when a number's tag turns gold and shows its symbol (! % &…), only Shift + that key hooks it.
