Snake 🐍

Eat food to grow, avoid crashing into walls, obstacles, or a body (yours or theirs). Turn on twists for teleport portals and obstacle mazes, or bring a friend for local 2-player.

Legend & twists
🍎 Food — eat it to grow one segment and speed up slightly.
▦ Obstacles — solid blocks scattered on the board; crashing into one ends your run.
🌀 Portals — two color-matched pairs (orange↔orange, purple↔purple); enter one end, exit the other end of the same color still moving the same direction. They relocate every few food eaten.
🐍🐍 2-Player — share the board and the food. Crash into a wall, obstacle, yourself, or the other snake and you're out. Head-on collisions kill both.
